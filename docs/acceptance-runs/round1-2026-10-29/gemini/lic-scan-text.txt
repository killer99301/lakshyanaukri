SELECTION PROCESS
Stage 1: Applications for apprenticeship will be invited during 20-10-2026 to 01-11-2026
Stage 2: Last Date to pay the examination fee to BFSI Sector Skill Council of India (https://beep.bfsissc.com): 01-11-2026
Stage 3: Entrance Examination will be conducted by BFSI Sector Skill Council of India on 07-11-2026
Stage 4: Candidates shortlisted based on the entrance examination result will be invited for Document Verification & Personal Interview at LIC HFL offices on 15-11-2026
Stage 5: Final selected candidates will be issued engagement letters by LIC HFL stating their Apprenticeship Training Branch, Monthly Stipend Payable, Rules / Regulations / Terms & Conditions related to the apprenticeship program of LIC HFL (Tentatively 3rd week of November)
Stage 6: Candidates who accept the offer letters will be asked to report to the respective LIC HFL branch for their apprenticeship training program (Tentatively 4th week of November)

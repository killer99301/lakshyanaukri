HOW TO APPLY AND OFFICIAL WEBSITE
Applications for apprenticeship will be invited (refer Table D for dates).
Candidates can apply online, and no other mode of application will be accepted. Candidates should first, mandatorily register themselves, if eligible, on the apprenticeship portals of Government of India viz NATS portal https://nats.education.gov.in.in (Navigate to the "Student Register/Login" section)
In case the candidates are facing issues in getting themselves registered they are advised to go through the help manual available on the respective apprenticeship portals. For NATS portal refer to "Candidate User Manual" on https://nats.education.gov.in/assets/manual/student_manual.pdf
Candidate will receive an email from BFSI SSC inviting the candidate to fill up a detailed application and make payment, for Online Examination for "LIC HFL Apprenticeship Program". Last date for payment for eligible candidates for online examination and submission of additional information at https://beep.bfsissc.com will be 01-Nov-2026
All candidates are advised to take note of their respective login id and passwords of the apprenticeship portals as applicable to them, as they will be repetitively visiting the apprenticeship portals for various activities till their completion of the apprenticeship program
For NATS portal, candidates can view the Apprenticeship Advertisement of "LICHFL" after login into the portal by visiting https://nats.education.gov.in/student_type.php. It is very IMPORTANT for all candidates who have applied to TAKE NOTE of their Enrolment Id (issued by NATS portal) for all the future correspondence.
All Candidates who have applied successfully for the Apprenticeship Opportunity/Advertisement on NATS will receive an email from BFSI SSC (info@updates.bfsissc.com) inviting them to give required details about the choice of districts for their training, etc. and make the payment of the Application Fees.
Official website: https://nats.education.gov.in
